{%- comment -%} The stack: the capability layers of _data/products.yml (seven: the state lifecycle of the paper plus evaluation), from acquiring a document to measuring what memory adds, each with its paper concept, product and research. The tile grid takes its column count from the data (--stack-n). Static baseline: an ordered list of the layers with everything on them. assets/js/components/stack.js replaces it with a pipeline of tabs (an accordion below 760 px) and product chips that mark each product's layers. Data: site.data.products (stack, products, statuses) and site.publications. Only the memory-layer products (those with `layers`) take part; the rest of the chain is not in the stack. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign pd = site.data.products -%} {%- assign stack_products = pd.products | where_exp: "p", "p.layers" -%} {%- assign products_url = '/products/' | relative_url -%} {%- assign slugs = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for layer in pd.stack -%}{%- assign slugs = slugs | concat: layer.research -%}{%- endfor -%} {%- assign slugs = slugs | uniq -%} {%- assign pub_json = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for slug in slugs -%} {%- assign rp = site.publications | where: "slug", slug | first -%} {%- if rp -%} {%- capture item -%}{{ slug | jsonify }}: {"title": {{ rp.title | jsonify }}, "venue": {{ rp.venue_short | jsonify }}, "url": {{ rp.url | relative_url | jsonify }}}{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign pub_json = pub_json | push: item -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign prod_json = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for p in stack_products -%} {%- capture item -%}{"id": {{ p.id | jsonify }}, "name": {{ p.name | jsonify }}, "status": {{ p.status | jsonify }}, "tagline": {{ p.tagline | jsonify }}, "layers": {{ p.layers | jsonify }}}{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign prod_json = prod_json | push: item -%} {%- endfor -%}
