§ 01 The problem

Today’s document AI has amnesia, by design.

Models now read contracts, extract fields and point to the page region behind each answer — then forget all of it. My 2026 survey with Ayoub Merimi names this the statelessness bottleneck and specifies what it takes to remove it.

Storage
gives persistence
Files and indexes outlive the session, but nothing is learned from them.
Retrieval
gives access
The right page can be found again, but each document is still read cold.
Consolidation
gives experience
Observations become provenance-linked knowledge that improves the next reading.
