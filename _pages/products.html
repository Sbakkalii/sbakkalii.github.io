§ 01 The stack

Seven layers, six products.

Read a document, gate what it says, consolidate it, reconcile it with what came before, keep the trail back to its source, forget it on request, and measure whether any of it helped. Each layer names the part of the paper it implements, the product that owns it, and the work it builds on.
